Hey, I'm Paige Boehmcke!

I'm passionate about helping organizations navigate change and build a more equitable economy. My work sits at the intersection of strategy, technology, and the future of work, with a particular interest in how AI and automation are reshaping organizations—and how leaders can respond in ways that expand opportunity.

At Bridgespan, I work with mission-driven organizations on strategy and organizational challenges. Before that, I was a researcher at Harvard Business School's Project on Managing the Future of Work, where I studied how technological change is transforming the workforce and contributed to research on what employers can do to prepare. Earlier in my career, I designed innovation programs at JFFLabs that supported entrepreneurs and organizations developing new workforce solutions. Along the way, I've been fortunate to work across consulting, research, startups, and nonprofit innovation, experiences that continue to shape how I approach complex problems.

I was also a Venture for America Fellow, an Education Pioneers Fellow, and I earned my MBA from MIT Sloan.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me advocating for more housing with JP YIMBY, mentoring college students through Partakers College Behind Bars, or training with Liberty Athletic Club. I also love live music and am a longtime fan of Boston's country music scene.

Happy to meet you!
